# Root.esp v2.5

1. **Kids achievement fixed.** Two causes of "free" achievements: *Just-right water* gave 1 star even when the pot overflowed (a fail), and the first badge (Seedling) unlocked at only 3 stars, so one game was enough. Now overflowing gives 0 stars, badges need 6 / 15 / 30 / 60 stars, and a "New badge" popup says exactly why it was earned. Stars are always added to the saved total (no lost or double-counted stars between tabs).
2. **Calm music for kids.** A soft, looping music-box lullaby made for Root.esp (`assets/sound/kids-calm-loop.mp3`) plays in the kids app with gentle fade in/out, pauses when the app is in the background and stops on sign-out. Music button on kids Home and My Plants; the choice is remembered per child. Uses `expo-av` (new dependency).
3. **Design photos are never cut.** Every history thumbnail and every designer picture (uploaded photo, outcome, preset, AI result) is shown whole on the same warm "photo mat", in frames of one size, so rooms and pot renders look like one gallery.
4. **Questionnaire is optional and moved.** The intro is now a short welcome only. The 6 design questions are offered in the Design studio once the person has looked around (after closing a design from history, or after ~20 s on the page), once, with "Not now". They can be answered or edited any time from "Your design answers".
5. **Transitions and loading page.** A "Preparing your garden" page (growing sprout, progress line, rotating messages; kids version "Hi Maya!") before entering the app after sign-in, account switch or a restored session. Tabs cross-fade, stack pages slide in, every page eases in, buttons press in. Animated splash.
6. **Polish.** New welcome screen, softer cards, consistent button feedback, kids music button, badge popup.
7. **Link-a-device prompt + Root.esp ads.** After an adult account has tried all five features (Home, My Plants, Design, Assistant, Profile) with no plant linked to a device, a popup asks to link one. *Yes* → Device management. *No* → one full Root.esp ad, then "Prototype ad · Root.esp" cards on Home, My Plants and Design (Smart Pot, Sensor Kit, Design studio). Ads stop once a plant is linked. Try it with `dan@sera.app` / `demopassword`.
8. **APK with Expo.** `eas.json` (`apk` profile → installable .apk, demo mode), Android package `com.rootesp.app`, versionCode 25, scripts `npm run apk`, `apk:local`, `apk:gradle`. Step-by-step guide: `Frontend/BUILD_APK.md`.

**Before the first run after updating:** in `Frontend/`, run `npm install` once (not `npm ci`) so `expo-av` is installed and `package-lock.json` is updated.

Tests: `node tests/redesign-run-all.cjs` adds `tests/v2-5-feature-test.cjs`.

## v2.5.3 (APK fixes)
- Icons are now vector shapes drawn with `react-native-svg` (`src/Icon.js`, paths in `src/iconPaths.js`, generated by `tools/build-icons.py`). They no longer depend on an icon font, which was not showing in the Android APK (empty squares, blank tab bar, no sprout on the loading page).
- Tabs switch without the cross-fade that could stop half way on Android; pages ease in on every visit and always end fully visible; the loading page always closes.
- Welcome banner sits below the header on phones; Android navigation bar uses the system default again (works with dark themes).
